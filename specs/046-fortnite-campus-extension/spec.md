## Requirements

- R01: Give each remaining primary campus area a meaningful visual improvement using installed Fortnite architectural meshes and original materials. Cover neutral floor slabs, circulation and existing structural surfaces. Preserve the accepted 045 work.

Retain existing authentic Fortnite prefabs rather than replacing them with another kit merely to increase change count.
- R05: Cover all audited primary buildings and shared circulation, and explicitly account for every remaining area and exclusion. Do not silently defer an entire primitive lab. Use the exact scene delta and coverage inventory, linked by hashes into map.yaml.
- R06: Save and verify the editor result against the delta. Supervisor reviews the concrete plan under the owner's continuing delegation. No automated gameplay tests, project validation, cook, push or session launch; owner walkthrough and runtime/memory acceptance remain pending.
