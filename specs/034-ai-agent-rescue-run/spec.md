# Pix's Rescue Run

Status: proposed design; no human approval or implementation acceptance.

Replace the old AI Agent Mission's repeated command-editing stations on floors 1–3 with a short, non-combat escort adventure: dispatch Pix with medical supplies, follow it to a surprise route closure, help it replan, then inspect the delivered crate at Animal Research Station. The player is Pix's supervisor: give a goal, let the agent act, intervene when conditions change, and check the result. Five decisions span three physical beats; the player moves beside a visible companion rather than answering five questions in one place. Retain the existing seven-module prerequisite and AI Agent Badge identity. Floor 4 and its legacy station remain outside the scene delta.

## Requirements

- R-01: Retain the requested bot floor structures and safe campus access. Reconcile the exact affected actor set before removal; do not treat a bot-name search as a deletion list. Replace obsolete local controls and duplicate games with one rescue exhibit, preserving shared progress, tracker, journal, spawners, hub, loadout, neighboring labs and structural shell.
- R-02: Entering the bay shows the short goal and unlock state automatically. Until the journal reports all seven prerequisite modules, choices remain inactive and the next missing module is named. Return remains available. Eligible entry starts after a two-second captioned Pix wake-up; no Claim/Run/Next cycle.
- R-03: Each of three physical beats has three fixed 1.5 m damage-only hit faces with letter, icon and word labels (nine total). Only the current beat's bank is active. Each phase presents exactly three choices from the table below, without pre-highlighting the right answer. Correct actions show a concrete consequence before advancing; wrong choices explain the unmet goal and retry within two seconds, without damage or loss of prior phase progress. Walking forward cannot skip a decision.
- R-04: The rescue uses authored local simulation. Delivery begins on the direct route, then a striped safety gate closes ahead of Pix; Pix stops before it, retaining the crate. Scanning reveals the alternative DOCK route. Replanning resumes the same Pickup -> Move -> Drop skill, with the parcel visibly carried and deposited at the lab. Unknown route information stays unknown until scanning. Visible parcel, Pix and route states must agree with text. Pix waits at each beat until the owner is within its 6 m interaction area; no race or following failure. No live AI service, AI navigation, combat, vehicle or off-island dependency.
- R-05: The final answer requires matching observed evidence to the goal. Only MEDICAL AT LAB, after verified successful delivery and a fresh deliberate shot, awards the AI Agent Badge. A command request or reassuring message is not proof. No badge before this check; reuse the existing badge guard and journal identity.
- R-06: Input is attributed to the owning player, locked during effects, and rearms only after 0.5 seconds without hits. Eight seconds idle offers a useful hint. Essential cues work with audio muted; text is brief and readable from standing/crouched play. No time bonus, timer failure or precision penalty.
- R-07: Replay/reentry clears the whole rescue attempt and restores props, retaining earned badges. Return, leave, death/respawn, disconnect and round reset cancel ongoing actions by generation. Round reset clears badge through existing lifecycle. One player owns the exhibit at a time; another player cannot answer or change its state. Do not change island matchmaking settings for this feature.
- R-08: Successful verification plays a short captioned AI Core celebration and holds the recap “Observe. Choose. Act. Check.” Offer Replay and Return; keep Return usable throughout. Typical first completion target is 60–120 seconds, measured in cooked play rather than assumed.

## Five decisions

Goal persists: **Deliver MEDICAL supplies to Animal Research Station.** Beat 1 is Dispatch on floor 1, beat 2 is Route Rescue on floor 2, and beat 3 is Delivery Check on floor 3. The two transfers are about 22 m and 24 m along a level, 3 m minimum clear path. Their purpose is following Pix and observing its changed situation. Pix never outruns the player: it pauses at the next beat until the owner arrives. Return switches at every beat avoid walking back to the hub; Replay at the finish returns the owner to Dispatch with the existing teleporter mechanism. This is not a player platforming bridge; the gate/detour is in the separate scripted robot lane, never across the player path.

| Phase | Prompt | A / B / C | Success and visible consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Dispatch: choose cargo | What does the research station need? | FOOD / MEDICAL / TOOLS | B: select and raise the Medical crate beside Pix; other crates stay unselected. |
| 2 Dispatch: launch skill | Which skill will deliver the crate? | DELIVER / LIGHT / ANNOUNCE | A: show Pickup -> Move -> Drop; Pix picks up the crate, travels to floor 2, then stops at the newly closed safety gate. Next cue: FOLLOW PIX. |
| 3 Route Rescue: observe | The route changed. Which tool helps? | SCAN / ANNOUNCE / FORCE THROUGH | A: sweep the route diagram; show BRIDGE BLOCKED / DOCK OPEN. Wrong actions cannot reveal route facts or force Pix through the gate. |
| 4 Route Rescue: replan | Use the scanner result. Which route works? | BRIDGE / WAIT / DOCK | C: illuminate the dock detour; resume the same skill, carrying the crate around the gate to the lab on floor 3. Next cue: CHECK THE DELIVERY. |
| 5 Check | Look at the lab. What proves the goal is met? | PIX SAID DONE / MEDICAL AT LAB / CRATE AT HOME | B: compare delivered crate identity and destination; only matching actual state completes the mission. |

Wrong choices do not perform a fake successful delivery or reveal unknown scan information. Wrong LIGHT explains that lighting does not move cargo, ANNOUNCE shows a caption, and WAIT briefly holds the scene. FORCE THROUGH and BRIDGE show the closed gate and explain why Pix stopped. Each result explicitly states what remains unmet. If required movement fails, restore the segment start and retry that segment's launch decision (phase 2 or 4), with “Delivery stopped—try again”, no semantic completion, verification or reward.

## Acceptance scenarios

- AC-01 (R-01/02): Given entry with fewer than seven modules, when the player approaches, then the lock and next missing module are readable, choices cannot advance, and Return works; after all seven modules, eligible entry starts automatically.
- AC-02 (R-03/04): Given a fresh eligible run, when MEDICAL then DELIVER are selected, then Pix carries the labelled crate to floor 2, the safety gate closes and Pix stops before it. Walking to later banks early cannot answer or skip phases; Pix waits if the owner lingers.
- AC-03 (R-04/05): Given Pix stopped at the changed route, when SCAN then DOCK are selected and the player follows, then the scan precedes a visible detour and Drop at floor 3. Only after both successful final transforms and owner arrival may verification appear. A failed or canceled segment produces no delivery credit.
- AC-04 (R-05/08): Given the crate visibly at the lab, when PIX SAID DONE or CRATE AT HOME is selected, then no badge is earned; MEDICAL AT LAB checks actual state and awards exactly once, then plays and holds the recap.
- AC-05 (R-03/06): Given each of five decisions, when either wrong choice is shot, then all ten wrong cases show a truthful mismatch, preserve earlier phases, and allow retry within two seconds. Scan facts remain unknown until successful scanning.
- AC-06 (R-06): Given held fire or rapid repeated hits through animation, when the next phase starts, then it waits for 0.5 seconds of quiet before accepting a new deliberate shot; no phase or badge duplicates. Inactive/other-player hits do not score.
- AC-07 (R-07): Given delivery in progress, when Return, leave, death/respawn, disconnect or round reset occurs, then late movement/credit cannot affect a new run. Replay restores all props and phase 1 while keeping a previously earned badge; round reset clears the badge.
- AC-08 (R-01/06/08): Given standing/crouched play and muted audio, when a first-time tester completes the run, then all labels and consequences are readable, hit faces match visuals, access and Return require no jump, old misleading controls are absent, and completion time/learning explanation/enjoyment are recorded.
- AC-09 (R-07): Given two players if supported by the current session settings, when the second shoots or enters, then only the owner can progress; owner departure cancels and releases the exhibit for a clean fresh attempt. Do not change matchmaking just to run this test; record an unavailable test explicitly.
- AC-10 (all): Given final bindings and saved source, when Verse build, project validation, cooking and the scenario matrix run, then there are no unresolved validation errors and each test has expected/actual evidence before its task is checked off.
- AC-11 (R-05): Given a validation fixture that displaces the delivered crate away from the lab or substitutes the wrong identity, when MEDICAL AT LAB is shot, then verification rejects and no badge is granted, even if the prior animation reported success. Restore a valid fixture and verify once. This is an implementation-only test fixture, not a player-facing extra mode.
