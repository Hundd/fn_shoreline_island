# Signal Station Grounding

## Requirements

### R1: Ground contact

Each signal station device (`signal_station_0` through `signal_station_3`) must sit on the top surface of its corresponding signal floor.

## Acceptance scenarios

### A1: Station placement

Given a player approaches any signal station, when the station and its floor are viewed together, then the station has no visible gap below it.

### A2: Consistent station layout

Given all four signal stations are inspected, when their Z locations are compared with their floor top surfaces, then every station uses the same grounded height.
