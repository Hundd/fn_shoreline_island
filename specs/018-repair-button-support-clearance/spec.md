# Repair Button Support Clearance

## Requirements

- R1: No repair control button may physically overlap its visual support mount.
- R2: Supports must remain visibly positioned behind the player-facing buttons.
- R3: Button transforms and device settings must remain unchanged.

## Acceptance scenarios

### S1: Clear interaction controls

Given a player approaches any repair control row,
when they target a button,
then its support mount does not obstruct or overlap the button.
