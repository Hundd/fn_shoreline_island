# Pattern Scanner: Cargo Circuit

Status: approved and implemented in UEFN; cooked gameplay acceptance remains open. Date: 2026-09-29.

Replace the four repeated Loop stations on loop_dock_0 through loop_dock_3 with one continuous, cooperative cargo-restoration game. Target first run: 3–5 minutes, solo or 2–4 players. Learning: observe examples, predict a repeating pattern, locate and repair an exception, and test a prediction against movement. These authored rules illustrate prediction; real AI predictions can be wrong and require checking evidence.

## Requirements

- FR-01: Use the measured 88 × 25 m dock footprint. Retain its four support floors and hub walkway; replace the old station gameplay, obsolete boards, controls, robots and local shed dressing. Keep a continuous 3 m accessible aisle and an always-available return. Remove actors through UEFN after dependency reconciliation, never by deleting content files.
- FR-02: Reuse the existing Data Blaster/Pulse Rifle and grant behavior, including safe non-combat play. The hub route sign points to the dock and tells players to press Start at its entrance. Arrival identifies the hub-side Start control and says “Watch the cargo. Shoot your prediction. Repair the scanner.” After Start, a five-second join message makes clear that the run has begun and the first pattern is about to appear. Start, Help, Watch again, Replay and Return are ordinary interactions; learning decisions use shooting. No mandatory reaction timer, resource cost, jump or death penalty.
- FR-03: Dock 0, Predict: demonstrate A-circle / B-triangle / A / B / A / ?. Three large answer carriers A-circle, B-triangle, C-square glide laterally at 0.5 m/s over a 3 m span. Shoot B to send the missing cargo into the line and power its first segment. Symbols and words accompany color. After two mistakes offer Slow/Freeze assistance; Help can freeze carriers immediately without reward loss. Incorrect hits show the repeating pair and allow another shot.
- FR-04: Dock 1, Repair: six crates move into numbered inspection slots showing A / B / A / A / A / B, then stop. Shoot slot 4, the exception to alternating A/B; it lifts into a repair cradle. Then shoot B from three replacement choices A/B/C. The repaired crate lowers, the correct A/B sequence runs through the scanner, and the second segment powers. Wrong slot or replacement keeps this stage and offers a specific explanation.
- FR-05: Dock 2 into Dock 3, Predict motion: a shuttle demonstrates stops 1,2,3,4,3,2, then waits at 2. Its direction arrow points toward 1 and its full trail remains visible. Shoot the predicted next stop from beacons 1,3,4. A correct shot at 1 moves the shuttle there, loads the repaired cargo and opens the dispatch shutter; a wrong guess shows the direction rule without consuming progress. Repeat observation is always available. No moving platform carries the player.
- FR-06: One shared run and physical machine, with cooperative answers accepted from enrolled players. Start opens a 5-second join window; entrants during it join the run. After it closes, newcomers can watch and join the next run. Clearly show “Team run” and stage 1/3–3/3. Every enrolled, still-present player receives stage credit when the team solves it; individual shooting is not required. This deliberately replaces feature 002's four independent stations. Outsider shots cannot alter the run. Replay cannot interrupt an active team run.
- FR-07: Retain loop_progress, loop_badge_tracker and their journal/finale bindings. Award the Pattern Badge once per player per round only after all three stages; replay retains earned badges. A partial player rejoining a later run starts visually at stage 1; only advance saved progress in order. No new currency reward is proposed. Finale sends the cargo through the raised shutter with a brief light/audio sequence and the recap “Spot the rule. Check the exception. Test your prediction.”
- FR-08: Every transition locks accepted inputs before animation, rejects duplicate/stale hits, and disables unrelated targets. Departure, death/respawn or disconnect removes that player from enrollment; already-earned progress remains. If no enrolled players remain, cancel motion and reset the physical line. Round reset cancels all pending work and clears progress. Replay resets props, hit surfaces, stage, hints and participants, preserving earned badges. No idle owner can hold a station.
- FR-09: Build a finished coastal Fortnite cargo depot: native conveyor bodies, crates, industrial machinery, lights, shelving, railings and limited planters. Use cyan scanner accents, warm practical lights, neutral metal and restrained yellow hazard trim. Put decoration at edges, keep targets readable and the sea visible, and build a recognizable entry and dispatch silhouette. At least 75% of non-device visible prop instances should use qualified Fortnite catalog assets; custom primitives are reserved for symbols, trim and necessary supports. Catalog discovery alone does not prove UEFN asset eligibility.
- FR-10: All targets are at least 1.2 m across with matching hit surfaces, 2 m or more center spacing, and labels above muzzle height. Use stage-local world text and a HUD copy. Essential information works with muted audio and without color distinction. Validate weapon traces and collisions in a cooked session; finish with UEFN open and no running game.

## Scope decisions

“Armor” is interpreted as the already-used shooting weapon, the Pulse Rifle. No wearable armor system is proposed. Four tiles remain structurally; permission to remove old assets applies to their obsolete activity and dressing. Separate field-note actors and the overlapping campus promenade are shared dependencies, not automatic removals. This proposal supersedes the old Loop interaction requirements only after approval and implementation.
