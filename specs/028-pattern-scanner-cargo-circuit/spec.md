## Player experience

Walk from the hub onto the first dock tile. A short sign says **SHOOT THE MISSING SYMBOL**. A persistent six-symbol strip has one obvious `?`. Shoot one of three large stationary answers: A / circle, B / triangle, C / square. The chosen correct symbol fills the gap, the whole pattern briefly highlights, and one of three progress lights turns on. The next puzzle appears in the same place. Three solved puzzles earn the existing Pattern Badge. Replay and Return sit beside the player.

Single-player only, as explicitly requested on 2026-09-29. Remove team enrollment, join delays, shared answers, spectators and cooperative rewards. This feature controls only this activity; changing island-wide matchmaking or other missions is outside this review scope.

Target first run: 45-90 seconds, without a time limit. The first answer should be attempted within 10 seconds of reaching the bay. These are playtest targets, not measured outcomes. Fun comes from immediately seeing a guess complete a pattern, a small increase in difficulty, and a clear three-step finish.

## Requirements

- S-01 ? One compact bay: place the entire game on dock 0, within local X=0..22, Y=0..25 m. Retain all four structural dock floors, hub walkway and shared routes. Arrival to the comfortable firing point is at most 10 m; no walking between rounds. Maintain a 3 m unobstructed route and always-available hub return.
- S-02 ? One action: all three puzzles use the same three stationary 1.5 m answer surfaces, centered 3 m apart and 6 m in front of the firing point. Each is labelled with both its letter and symbol. The persistent pattern, question and answers fit one forward view. Reuse the existing Pulse Rifle and grant/respawn path. No tracking, memorization, Start button, countdown, Watch Again or Freeze control.
- S-03 ? Three rounds: (1) `A B A B A ?` -> B; (2) `A B ? B A B` -> A; (3) `A B C A B ?` -> C. Render symbols alongside these letters. Round 2 keeps repair learning as filling an interior gap; remove exception hunting and the separate replacement phase. Round 3 tests a new repeating group; remove shuttle direction/stop prediction. Each round has exactly one correct answer and grants ordered progress 0, 1, 2.
- S-04 ? Immediate feedback: wrong shots preserve the puzzle and progress and show a specific rule hint for up to 3 seconds. After the second wrong answer, highlight the repeating group and name the correct symbol; the player still shoots it. Correct shots lock the round immediately, fill the gap within 0.25 seconds, show the completed strip and a brief pulse for 1 second, then expose the next puzzle after a total 1.5-second transition. No cargo travel or machinery waiting. Essential feedback is visible with muted audio and without color distinction.
- S-05 ? Solo lifecycle: when the sole local player enters the play bay, the first puzzle becomes ready within 0.5 seconds. Only that player's in-bay attributed shots can advance it. Departure, death/respawn, disconnect or round reset cancels pending callbacks and resets the physical puzzle. Reentry begins at puzzle 1; previously earned ordered progress and badges persist until round reset. No team, join window, spectator messaging or multiplayer acceptance requirement.
- S-06 ? Minimal controls and finish: exactly two local ordinary controls, Replay and Return. Replay is enabled only after completion; it resets all three rounds and hints without awarding another badge. Return works at any time. Hints are automatic. Completion holds `3/3 ? PATTERN BADGE EARNED` and a short recap until Replay/departure. Use the retained loop_progress/tracker identity and existing journal/finale bindings; no new currency.
- S-07 ? Every prominent object has a role: remove all 21 cargo_circuit_conveyor instances, all cargo carriers/inspection crates/delivery/shuttle/shutter, old stop signs/arrow, excess target assemblies, old instructions and redundant controls. Remove eight decorative crate stacks and two planters. Retain/reposition only boundary rails needed for edge safety and at most two existing practical lamps near the bay. Keep the existing support canopy only where it shelters the bay and does not obstruct the view; remove dock-owned excess canopy dressing after dependency checks. No prop quota. Shared field notes, promenade and structural supports are protected. No fake conveyor/scanner interaction remains.
- S-08 ? Preserve clarity and safety: exactly three visible/shootable answers, one six-slot instruction board, three progress lights and a short hub direction sign. Existing ring cues identify shootable answers; examples have no rings or hit surfaces. No target-obscuring props, decorative objectives or new moving hazards. ## Scope and tradeoffs

This intentionally replaces v1's industrial restoration story and movement-prediction lesson with one consistent pattern-completion mechanic. Three fixed puzzles prioritize a clear first play; procedural variety and timed scores are not part of this revision. Single-player refers to this mission; this change does not alter shared island matchmaking settings. The visible second/third/fourth tiles remain safe dock space, without obsolete machinery implying another task.
