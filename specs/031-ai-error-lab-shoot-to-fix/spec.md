# AI Error Lab: Fix Pix's Route

Revision 1, approved by the project owner on 2026-09-30. Supersedes feature 006's four-station gameplay in this location and feature 022's presentation. Editor implementation and build evidence are recorded; cooked gameplay acceptance remains pending. Single-player only, as requested; multiplayer is outside this feature's implementation and acceptance scope.

## Experience

Walk into the existing first lab tile with the shared Pulse Rifle. A small Pix marker runs a short faulty plan on a raised numbered five-tile display. Goal and actual result remain visible together. The board says **FIX THE BOXED STEP - SHOOT LEFT, STOP OR RIGHT**. Shoot a large stationary correction target: the boxed instruction changes, Pix returns to the start, reruns the entire plan and visibly stops at the resulting tile. A match lights one progress indicator; three repairs earn the existing AI Detective Badge. Wrong corrections show their actual result and allow another shot with no penalty.

One place, one action, three short rounds. LEFT moves one tile toward the player's left, STOP stays on the same tile for that instruction, RIGHT moves one tile toward the player's right. All other instructions are fixed. STOP means a zero-distance step, not pause, cancel, replay or end the program. No direction/count/category editing modes. No countdown or moving aim. First-run target 45-90 seconds; first correction attempted within 10 seconds of entering. These are future playtest targets, not measured results.

| Round | Start tile | Goal | Faulty plan (boxed slot) | Initial actual | Correct shot | Verified result |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---:|
| 1: Wrong way | 0 | 3 | RIGHT, [LEFT], RIGHT | 1 | RIGHT | 3 |
| 2: Back to the dock | 2 | 1 | [RIGHT] | 3 | LEFT | 1 |
| 3: Don't overshoot | 1 | 2 | RIGHT, [RIGHT] | 3 | STOP | 2 |

Tile numbering increases left to right from the firing point. Every possible correction keeps every intermediate position within tiles 0..4. Exactly one of LEFT/STOP/RIGHT reaches the goal in each round. Successful shots are IDs [2,0,1]. There is no separate Verify input: completion requires the actual rerun endpoint to match the goal, not merely a hardcoded answer-ID hit. The recap states **Check the goal. Change one mistake. Run again and check the result.** These are authored examples of checking AI plans, not real model inference or a claim that AI is always wrong.

## Requirements

- R-01 **Compact existing location.** Required play fits first floor world X=-4600..-1800, Y=-7800..-4100, top Z=2400 cm. Proposed bay is local X=0..24, Y=0..37 m with a 4 m entry strip; origin [-4600,-7800,2400]. Arrival (25,35) to firing point (14,27) is about 13.6 m; no travel between rounds. Keep a 3 m unobstructed route and reversible walking exit. Preserve structural floors, walkway, neighboring Confidence Core and shared hub infrastructure.
- R-02 **Readable stationary shooting.** Exactly three stationary 1.5 m hit faces at (11,21,1.5), (14,21,1.5), (17,21,1.5); 3 m center spacing, 6 m ahead. LEFT/left arrow, STOP/square, RIGHT/right arrow use permanent text plus symbols. Cyan rings indicate only these selectable correction targets. Shared rifle grant/respawn path remains. Pix, goal and track have no hit surfaces. Plan/result board at (14,16,5.3), track tile centers X=10,12,14,16,18 at Y=18, display height Z=3 m. Labels below target faces must not cover the board or marker. Player looks toward -Y; LEFT means decreasing world/local X.
- R-03 **Actual debugging.** Execute the exact table above. Automatically demonstrate each faulty plan once before arming corrections; keep GOAL, BEFORE and CURRENT/AFTER values visible. Highlight only the editable boxed instruction and current execution step. Each shot replaces that slot and reruns from its authored start. Only a verified endpoint match completes a round. Goal and command meanings never change within a round. One numbered track and one goal marker update between rounds.
- R-04 **Responsive safe retries.** Acknowledge an accepted correction within 0.25 s; run at 0.5 s per instruction (including STOP), so a rerun lasts at most 1.5 s. Show a result for at least 1 s. On mismatch keep progress unchanged and give an endpoint-specific explanation; after two distinct mistakes in the round name the useful correction. Hints persist until the next accepted action/reset. Lock scoring during demonstration, rerun and transitions; observe hits while locked and require 0.5 s of quiet before arming the next round. No held-fire credit across rounds, damage, lives, score loss or restart on a wrong answer. Effects last <=1 s and do not obscure evidence.
- R-05 **Solo lifecycle.** Detect the sole local player's entry within 0.5 s; first automatic demonstration begins immediately, then choices arm. Only that player's attributed in-bay shots count. No Claim/Start/Join, team ownership, spectator UI or multiplayer tests. Leave, death/respawn, disconnect and round reset cancel all pending callbacks, clear transient edits/hints, reset Pix and disable choices. Reentry/replay begins round 1; earned completed flags and badge persist until manager round reset. Local displayed progress counts this run, independent of retained badge progress.
- R-06 **Finish and progress.** Three lights and 0/3..3/3 text. Reuse debug_progress and debug_station_1_badge_tracker; complete indices 0,1,2 only after verified matches, retain existing journal/Agent Mission prerequisite/restoration identity. Exactly two ordinary local controls: Replay after completion and Return at any time; automatic hints and round advance. Replay starts a new physical run without clearing earned badge or issuing a second award. Finish holds badge status and recap until replay/departure; do not introduce currency.
- R-07 **Retire old activity.** Remove all four legacy debug_station controllers and their old claim/edit/run/help/next flows. Reuse station 1 Replay/Return, boards, HUD and marker where eligible; remove the other 26 old buttons, obsolete button labels, duplicate station boards/markers/tile labels and cargo/category props. Replace old Garden/Storage lesson signs. Exact actor/component/dependency reconciliation precedes removals. Preserve debug_progress, tracker, shared spawners, round settings, hub destination, all structural/debug-labelled support floors, walkway and shared scenery/routes. Reduce lab-owned console furniture only where obsolete collision or misleading controls remain. Never remove by label substring or edit binary files manually.
- R-08 **Visual purpose.** A visible numbered tabletop, Pix marker, goal marker, three correction targets, one plan/result board, three progress indicators, Replay/Return and short entry instructions form the complete game. A useful shelter/edge rails and at most two practical lamps may remain. No replacement decorative quota or imported art. Every required cue works without sound or color; do not hide the goal/actual evidence behind target labels or bright effects.
- R-09 **Evidence before acceptance.** Build Verse, validate project, cook, then test the real solo rifle route, every correction, hints, held fire, lifecycle, badge/journal/Agent Mission recognition and muted-audio readability. ## Scope

No other mission redesign, global matchmaking change, multiplayer support, procedural levels, competitive scoring, publishing or cross-session persistence. Detailed furniture identities and native properties are implementation preflight work under this design, not authorization to change the intended layout.
