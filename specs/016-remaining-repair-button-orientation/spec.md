# Remaining Repair Button Orientation

## Requirements

- R1: Every interactable control button in the `repair_2` and `repair_3` rows must face the player approach side.
- R2: Button positions, scales, labels, and device settings must remain unchanged.

## Acceptance scenarios

### S1: Consistent repair controls

Given a player approaches either remaining repair-control row from its label side,
when they view any control,
then its usable front faces the player.
